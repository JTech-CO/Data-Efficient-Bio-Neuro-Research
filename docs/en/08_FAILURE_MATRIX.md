# Failure → diagnostic → remedy → residual limitation

Remedies combine established methods with proposed operational safeguards. This is not evidence that every combination has been validated.

| Failure | Cause | Diagnostic | Remedy | Remaining limit | Sources |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| GP overconfidence | Wrong kernel, noise or group assumptions | Coverage/width, kernel sensitivity, new-group residuals | Hierarchical/robust likelihoods, prior sensitivity, independent exploration | No automatic arbitrary-shift guarantee | [R01](../../references/BIBLIOGRAPHY.md#r01) [R23](../../references/BIBLIOGRAPHY.md#r23) |
| Acquisition lock-in | Confusing uncertainty with target information | Repeated/noisy queries and cost versus random | Target information, replicate costs, exploration controls | Shared blind spots across all candidates | [R02](../../references/BIBLIOGRAPHY.md#r02) [R05](../../references/BIBLIOGRAPHY.md#r05) |
| Noisy SINDy | Point differentiation amplifies measurement noise | Noise sweep and coefficient stability | Weak/integral forms and ensembles | Hidden state and incorrect library remain | [R04](../../references/BIBLIOGRAPHY.md#r04) [R05](../../references/BIBLIOGRAPHY.md#r05) |
| Compact but misleading equations | Unchecked alternatives, dimensions and identifiability | Units, posterior/profile ridges, intervention rollout | Identifiable reparameterization and equation cards | Interpretability is not causality | [R06](../../references/BIBLIOGRAPHY.md#r06) [T02](../../references/BIBLIOGRAPHY.md#t02) |
| Strong wrong physics | Treating empirical assumptions as exact laws | Wrong-prior stress and structured residuals | Hard constraints only where justified; shrunk discrepancy | Residuals may obscure mechanistic parameters | [R10](../../references/BIBLIOGRAPHY.md#r10) [R27](../../references/BIBLIOGRAPHY.md#r27) |
| Overcertain preprocessing | Treating masks or imputation as unquestioned observations | Raw/derived comparison and downstream sensitivity | Observation layers and uncertainty propagation | Ensembles may still be miscalibrated | [R07](../../references/BIBLIOGRAPHY.md#r07) [R15](../../references/BIBLIOGRAPHY.md#r15) |
| Foundation negative transfer | Representation objective differs from downstream task | Equal-setting raw/linear/frozen/adapter comparisons | Staged adaptation and improvement gates | More pretraining is not automatic benefit | [R11](../../references/BIBLIOGRAPHY.md#r11) [R12](../../references/BIBLIOGRAPHY.md#r12) [R13](../../references/BIBLIOGRAPHY.md#r13) |
| Evaluation leakage | Group/session/pretraining overlap | Hashes, group manifests and corpus audit | Group splits, external holdout, declared overlap tracks | Undisclosed pretraining may remain unknown | [R18](../../references/BIBLIOGRAPHY.md#r18) [D01](../../references/BIBLIOGRAPHY.md#d01) |
| Invalid fidelity ordering | Treating different domains as accuracy levels | Paired-bridge residuals and high-only comparison | Discrepancy or multitask domain model | Few bridge samples may not identify transfer | [R19](../../references/BIBLIOGRAPHY.md#r19) [R20](../../references/BIBLIOGRAPHY.md#r20) |
| Synthetic circularity | Reusing model outputs as independent evidence | Real-only tests, lineage, source-label dependence | Train-only generation and augmentation ablations | Provenance alone does not guarantee utility | [R09](../../references/BIBLIOGRAPHY.md#r09) [R21](../../references/BIBLIOGRAPHY.md#r21) |
| Synthetic privacy assumptions | Assuming generation removes individual information | Appropriate privacy attacks and proximity checks | Access control; DP where appropriate with utility tests | Anonymization and DP are different guarantees | [R22](../../references/BIBLIOGRAPHY.md#r22) |
| Single-fit mechanistic claims | Collapsing compatible parameters into one optimum | Multiple posterior modes and predictive checks | SBI, hierarchical inference or partial identification | Simulator bias and computational cost | [R25](../../references/BIBLIOGRAPHY.md#r25) [R26](../../references/BIBLIOGRAPHY.md#r26) |
