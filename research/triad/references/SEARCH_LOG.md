## Selection
Selected measurement commutability, the specific standard-addition limitation, spatial detection/cost, replication/noise estimation, robust likelihood inference, proper scoring, and sequential monitoring conditions. Excluded tangential applications and unverified 2026 search-only claims from conclusions. DOI and journal publication years were preferred over preprint years. T05's Consensus year (2016) was corrected to journal year 2018; T04 uses journal year 2019 rather than online date 2018. Complete copyright texts, original figures, datasets and weights are not redistributed.

## Coverage limitation
Primary abstracts and metadata were inspected, not every full text. Some publisher/PubMed direct opens were blocked; search-index text and fetched Consensus metadata were used for those records. Implementation equations and synthetic results in this version are our own, separately tested. No citation is evidence that our particular implementation or real biological transfer works.
